# [Louis L'Amour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_L%27Amour)
Louis Dearborn L'Amour (March 22, 1908 – June 10, 1988) was an American novelist and short story writer. His books consisted primarily of Western novels, though he called his work "frontier stories".

- *Bowdrie*
- *The Californios*
- *The Key-Lock Man*
- *Chancy*
- *The Haunted Mesa*
- *Lonesome Gods*
- *To Tame a Land*
- *War Party*
- *The Rider of the Ruby Hills*
- *Dutchman's Flat*
- *The Strong Shall Live*
- *Last of the Breed*
- *The High Graders*
- *The Broken Gun*
- *Lonigan*
- *The Outlaws of Mesquite*
- *Rivers West*

Recurring Locations from Louis L'Amour novels:

- Mule Canyon
- Cedar Mesa
- Canyon De Chelly
- Hovenweep
- Three Room Ruin

# [Elmore Leonard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmore_Leonard)
Elmore John Leonard Jr. (October 11, 1925 – August 20, 2013) was an American novelist, short story author and screenwriter. His earliest novels, published in the 1950s, were Westerns, but he went on to specialize in crime fiction and suspense thrillers, many of which have been adapted into motion pictures.

- *The Bounty Hunters*
- *Freaky Deaky*

# [Poul Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poul_Anderson)
Poul William Anderson (November 25, 1926 – July 31, 2001) was an American fantasy and science fiction author who was active from the 1940s until his death in 2001. Anderson also wrote historical novels.

- *The High Crusade*

# [James Ellroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ellroy)
Lee Earle "James" Ellroy (born March 4, 1948) is an American crime fiction writer and essayist.

- *The Black Dahlia*

# [Daniel Keyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Keyes)
Daniel Keyes (August 9, 1927 – June 15, 2014) was an American writer best known as the author of the novel *Flowers for Algernon*. Keyes was given the Author Emeritus honor by the Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America in 2000.

- *The Minds of Billy Milligan*

# [Dick Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Francis)
Richard Stanley Francis (31 October 1920 – 14 February 2010) was a British steeplechase jockey and crime writer whose novels center on horse racing in England.

- *Dead Cert*
- *Longshot*

# [David L. Lindsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_L._Lindsey)
David L. Lindsey (born 1944) is an American novelist, working primarily in the mystery and crime fiction genres.

- *A Cold Mind*
- *Mercy*

# [John Grisham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Grisham)
John Ray Grisham Jr. (born February 8, 1955) is an American writer, lawyer, and former politician, known for his best-selling legal thrillers.

- *The Pelican Brief*
- *The Firm*
- *The Client*

# [John Sandford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Sandford_(novelist))
John Roswell Camp (born February 23, 1944), known by the pen name John Sandford, is an American journalist and novelist. He works primarily in the crime fiction and thriller genres.

- *Silent Prey*
- *Rules of Prey*
- *Eyes of Prey*
- *Winter Prey*

# [Dean Koontz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Koontz)
Dean Ray Koontz (born July 9, 1945) is an American author. His novels are billed as suspense thrillers, but frequently incorporate elements of horror, fantasy, science fiction, mystery, and satire. 

- *Cold Fire*
- *Shadow Fires*
- *Phantoms*
- *Night Chills*
- *The Face of Fear*
- *Hideaway*
- *Midnight*
- *The Voice of the Night*
- *Whispers*

# [Larry McMurtry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larry_McMurtry)
Larry Jeff McMurtry (June 3, 1936 – March 25, 2021) was an American novelist, essayist, and screenwriter whose work was predominantly set in either the Old West or contemporary Texas.

- *Some Can Whistle*
- *The Desert Rose*

# [Pat Conroy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pat_Conroy)
Donald Patrick Conroy (October 26, 1945 – March 4, 2016) was an American author who wrote several acclaimed novels and memoirs. He is recognized as a leading figure of late-20th-century American Southern literature.

- *The Water is Wide*
- *The Prince of Tides*
- *The Lords of Discipline*

# [Tony Hillerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Hillerman)
Anthony Grove Hillerman (May 27, 1925 – October 26, 2008) was an American author of detective novels and nonfiction works, best known for his mystery novels featuring the fictional Navajo Nation Police officers Joe Leaphorn and Jim Chee.

- *Talking God*
- *A Thief of Time*
- *Skinwalkers*
- *The Ghostway*
- *The Dark Wind*
- *People of Darkness*
- *Listening Woman*
- *Dance Hall of the Dead*
- *The Fly on the Wall*
- *The Blessing Way*
- *Coyote Waits*
- *Sacred Clowns*

# [William Bayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Bayer)
William Bayer (pronounced “byer”) is an American novelist, the author of twenty-one books including The New York Times best-sellers *Switch* and *Pattern Crimes*. Bayer's novels fall into the category of psychological crime fiction, and several have been described as "neo-noir."

- *Wallflower*

# [Michael Crichton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Crichton)
John Michael Crichton (October 23, 1942 – November 4, 2008) was an American author and filmmaker. His books have sold over 200 million copies worldwide, and over a dozen have been adapted into films. His literary works heavily feature technology and are usually within the science fiction, techno-thriller, and medical fiction genres.

- *Congo*
- *Rising Sun*
- *Jurassic Park*
- *Sphere*
- *Eaters of the Dead*
- *Binary*

# [Clive Cussler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clive_Cussler)
Clive Eric Cussler (July 15, 1931 – February 24, 2020) was an American adventure novelist and underwater explorer. His thriller novels, many featuring the character Dirk Pitt, have been listed on The New York Times fiction best-seller list more than 20 times. 

- *Iceberg*
- *Raise the Titanic!*
- *Vixen 03*
- *Night Probe!*
- *Cyclops*
- *Treasure*
- *Dragon*
- *Sahara*
- *Deep Six*
- *Pacific Vortex!*
- *Inca Gold*
- *Mayday! / The Mediterranean Caper*

# [Kurt Vonnegut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_Vonnegut)
Kurt Vonnegut (November 11, 1922 – April 11, 2007) was an American author known for his satirical and darkly humorous novels.

- *The Sirens of Titan*
- *Cat's Cradle*
- *Slaughterhouse-Five*
- *Welcome to the Monkey House*
- *Breakfast of Champions*
- *Slapstick*
- *Jailbird*
- *Deadeye Dick*
- *Galápagos*
- *Hocus Pocus*

# [Nelson DeMille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_DeMille)
Nelson Richard DeMille (August 23, 1943 – September 17, 2024) was an American author of action adventure and suspense novels. 

- *The Talbot Odyssey*
- *Word of Honor*
- *The General's Daughter*
